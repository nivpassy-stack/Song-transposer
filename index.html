    Songs

      Add songs

      Paste one or many songs, or choose files (.txt, ChordPro, PDF, Word). You can also drag files onto the page at any time. Each file becomes its own setlist.

      Edit song

      This is the song in its original key. To split two songs that were read as one, put the cursor on the second song's title line and choose Split here.

      Back up library

      This is your whole library: every setlist and song, with its key and length. Save it as a file, or copy it, and use Restore a backup to bring it into the app on another device or browser.

      Restore a backup

      Choose a backup file, or paste a copied backup. Its setlists are added to your library; nothing you already have is removed.
